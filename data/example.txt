# A short tour of the engine. Prices are in paise: 10000 is Rs 100.00.

# Three sellers. Orders 1 and 2 are both at Rs 100.00; order 1 arrived first.
SELL 1 5 10000
SELL 2 5 10000
SELL 3 4 10100

# A buyer willing to pay up to Rs 100.50 buys 7: the cheapest first, and at the same price,
# the oldest first. Each trade is at the seller's price.
BUY 4 7 10050

# A buyer at Rs 99.00 that expires when the clock reaches 100.
BUY 5 3 9900 GTT 100
PRINT

# Fill Or Kill: 10 can't be bought at Rs 101.00 or less (only 7 are for sale), so nothing trades.
BUY 6 10 10100 FOK

# Time passes; order 5 expires.
TIME 100

# Mistakes are rejected with a reason, and nothing changes.
CANCEL 5
BUY 7 0 10000
PRINT
